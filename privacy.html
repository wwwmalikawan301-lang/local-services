Rabta - Privacy Policy

Rabta aik free app hai jo logon ko kaarigar, dukaan aur gariyon ke raaston se milati hai.

Hum kya jama karte hain

Jo aap khud add karein: naam, phone aur WhatsApp number, kaam ki tafseel, shehr, gaon/mohalla, aur tasveeren (apni aur kaam ki). Agar aap ijazat dein to taqreeban 1 km tak gol kiya hua location bhi.

Ye kaise istemal hota hai

Admin ki manzoori ke baad aap ka naam, number, tafseel aur tasveer app mein sab ko nazar aate hain, taake log aap se rabta kar saken. Isi liye add karte waqt aap se ijazat li jati hai. Hum ye data bechte nahi.

Data kahan rakha jata hai

Data Google Firebase par mehfooz hota hai. App aap ko ek anjaan (anonymous) ID deti hai, naam ya email nahi maangti.

Apna data hatana

Kisi bhi listing ke detail page par "Ye mera number hai, hata dein" dabayen, ya email karein. Hum number par rabta kar ke tasdeeq karte hain aur listing hata dete hain.
